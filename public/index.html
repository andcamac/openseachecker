Mint Check

Paste a wallet address. No connecting, no signing — it asks OpenSea's public API which open drops that address can mint right now.

The calendar scan covers what OpenSea lists as featured, upcoming, and recently minted. A live drop that's in none of those lists won't be found by the scan — check it directly with its URL. OpenSea also refuses to build a mint for a wallet that can't pay the price + gas on that chain — those show as "wallet needs funds", not as ineligible.
